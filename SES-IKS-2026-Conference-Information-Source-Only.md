# About the Conference

Environmental sustainability is one of the most pressing global challenges of our time. While modern science and technology have significantly advanced our understanding of environmental processes, there is growing recognition that sustainable solutions require integrating scientific innovation with traditional and indigenous knowledge systems.

This region possesses a rich heritage of environmental stewardship embedded within its Indigenous Knowledge Systems (IKS), where nature has traditionally been regarded as an integral part of human life and cultural practice.

Indigenous traditions such as:
- Rainwater harvesting
- Sacred groves
- Community forest management
- Sustainable agricultural practices

have historically contributed to:
- Ecological balance
- Resource conservation
- Sustainable living
- Ecological resilience

These time-tested practices demonstrate the potential of traditional wisdom in promoting sustainable living and ecological resilience.

However, increasing environmental challenges including:
- Climate change
- Water scarcity
- Biodiversity loss
- Emerging pollutants

demand innovative and interdisciplinary solutions that bridge knowledge systems.

While modern science and technology have greatly enhanced the ability to understand environmental processes and develop remediation strategies, integrating indigenous ecological knowledge with contemporary scientific approaches offers a holistic pathway toward sustainability.

The International Conference on Sustainable Environmental Solutions (SES-IKS 2026) aims to bring together scientists, academicians, policymakers, practitioners, and students to:
- Explore the synergy between traditional wisdom and contemporary scientific innovations
- Promote interdisciplinary collaboration
- Co-develop innovative and sustainable solutions

The conference will serve as a platform for:
- Knowledge exchange
- Partnership building
- Advancing actionable strategies for environmental sustainability

The conference poster describes SES-IKS 2026 as bringing together global experts, researchers, policymakers, practitioners, and students to co-create innovative, inclusive, and scalable solutions for a resilient planet.

# Abstract Submission Guidelines

The conference material contains the following abstract requirements.

## Formatting

- Times New Roman
- 12-point font
- 1.5 line spacing

## Content restrictions

The abstract should contain:
- No figures
- No tables
- No diagrams
- No formulas
- No references
- No special symbols

## Required sequence

**Title → Author(s) Name → Affiliation → Email → Abstract → Keywords**

The title should not exceed **25 words**.

## Author information

Full names, affiliations, and email addresses of all authors must be clearly mentioned.

## Research content

The abstract should clearly state:
- Research problem
- Objectives

- Key findings
